## What is the keybind to open the Click GUI

The default keybind is `INSERT` or if you're on mobile you, a button should appear called 'Click GUI'. You would press that to open. To rebind the Click GUI, you must invoke the command `.bind <module> <key>`, learn more at the commands section

## How to create a Module Button on mobile
You must open the 'Click GUI' (theres usually a button to open it) and hold the middle of a module and tap anywhere on the screen and it's created
